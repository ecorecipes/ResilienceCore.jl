Gallery: resilience across ecorecipes

ResilienceCore computes metrics on operators. The modelling packages produce those operators from their own models, and each one shows how in its own documentation. This page links to them, grouped by the kind of object the metrics are computed on. The table on the home page says which metrics apply to which object.

Simulation and SciML models

DynamicalResilience.jl brings ResilienceCore to SciML problems:

  • steady states, R₀, Floquet multipliers and continuation to a fold or Hopf point;
  • simulation-based recovery, basin stability and early-warning indicators, on SciML's integrator interface.

Structured populations

  • MatrixProjectionModels.jl and IntegralProjectionModels.jl: each defines linearise for its density-independent, deterministic problems. It gives a ProjectionMatrix, with mesh weights for an IPM.
  • FiniteStatePopulationDynamics.jl: generator problems give a Generator, the Jacobian at an affine equilibrium, or a PeriodicOperator (section).
  • ContinuousStatePopulationDynamics.jl: the same, with mesh weights (section).
