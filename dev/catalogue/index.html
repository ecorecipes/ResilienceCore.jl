The 27 metrics of Domínguez-García, Dakos & Kéfi (2019)

stability_profile computes all 27 for any community that implements the community interface. jacobian_profile gives the 11 that need only a Jacobian. Values keep their natural orientation; the paper flips signs so that larger always means more stable. The last column gives the paper's value in terms of ours (N is the number of species present).
